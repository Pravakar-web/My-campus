ABOUT OUR CAMPUS

A Vibrant 300-Acre Academic Hub in Kanakapura

Nestled amidst the serene, green foothills of Kanakapura, Karnataka, Jain Global Campus is a premier residential and research institution affiliated with JAIN (Deemed-to-be University). Designed to foster intellectual growth and holistic development, the campus houses the Faculty of Engineering & Technology (FET) alongside state-of-the-art research centers.

Our integrated academic ecosystem brings together world-class faculty, advanced industry-partnered laboratories, and experiential learning pedagogy to prepare students for successful careers in an evolving global landscape.
