Courses Offered

Industry-aligned academic degree programs offered at Jain Global Campus, Kanakapura

Engineering & Technology

B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering

Premier 4-year engineering program with cutting-edge specializations in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, and Full-Stack Development.

⏱️ Duration: 4 Years 🎯 Eligibility: 10+2 with PCM / JET
Aerospace & Aviation

B.Tech in Aerospace Engineering

Comprehensive program with world-class aerodynamics labs, flight simulation centers, UAV design, avionics systems, and propulsion engineering research.

⏱️ Duration: 4 Years 🎯 Eligibility: 10+2 with PCM
Computer Applications

BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)

Industry-oriented undergraduate program covering software engineering, modern web technologies, database administration, mobile app development, and Python programming.

⏱️ Duration: 3 Years 🎯 Eligibility: 10+2 with Math/CS
Electronics & VLSI

B.Tech in Electronics & Communication

Focused on Embedded Systems, Internet of Things (IoT), VLSI chip design, signal processing, next-generation 5G/6G wireless networks, and robotics.

⏱️ Duration: 4 Years 🎯 Eligibility: 10+2 with PCM
Design & Media

B.Des (Bachelor of Design)

Professional design degree emphasizing UI/UX interaction design, product design, digital media arts, spatial design, and user-centric creative innovation.

⏱️ Duration: 4 Years 🎯 Eligibility: 10+2 Any Stream / JET
Postgraduate Studies

MCA (Master of Computer Applications)

Advanced master's degree focusing on enterprise architecture, deep learning, distributed cloud systems, big data analytics, and corporate internships.

⏱️ Duration: 2 Years 🎯 Eligibility: BCA / B.Sc / Graduation
